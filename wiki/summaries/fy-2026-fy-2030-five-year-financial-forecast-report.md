Official **Five-Year Financial Forecast Report (FY 2026–FY 2030)** from the City of Austin **Office of Budget and Organizational Excellence**, dated **April 8, 2025** (55 pages). It projects operating revenues, expenditures, fund balances, full-time equivalents (FTEs), and **typical ratepayer or taxpayer bill impacts** for the **General Fund**, **enterprise departments** (Convention Center, Austin Energy, Austin Resource Recovery, Austin Water, Aviation, Development Services, Transportation and Public Works, Watershed Protection), the **Capital Improvement Program (CIP)** and **voter-approved general obligation (Public Improvement Bond) programs**, **American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA)** Coronavirus State and Local Fiscal Recovery Funds (SLFRF) status, **unfunded Council direction**, and **FY 2025 budget adoption amendments** that still need funding follow-up.

## Main takeaways

- **General Fund:** FY 2026 base expenditures are projected at about **$1.5 billion** (roughly **5.4%** above the FY 2025 Amended Budget), with **public safety** (Police, Fire, EMS, Forensic Science) receiving the largest share. The forecast shows **growing structural deficits** through FY 2030 under stated assumptions (including use of the **voter-approval tax rate** for property tax each year), with the **typical monthly property tax** portion for a typical homeowner rising from **$164.13** (FY 2025) to **$219.16** (FY 2030) in the summary table.
- **Revenue stress:** The narrative highlights a **sharp assumed decline in taxable property value** in FY 2026 (preliminary Travis Central Appraisal District indications), **soft sales tax** growth, and **conservative new-construction value** assumptions tied to higher interest rates and slower development.
- **Enterprise funds:** Each major enterprise section describes **operating revenue and expenditure drivers**, **transfers** (including to the General Fund where applicable), **CIP or debt**, and a **“typical” residential customer bill** trajectory through FY 2030.
- **CIP and debt:** Citywide CIP spending is framed historically and prospectively; active **PIB (GO bond) programs** (2016, 2018, 2020, 2022) are summarized with authorization, expended amounts, and **expected timing** of high spend-out shares.
- **ARPA:** Austin received **$188.5 million** in one-time SLFRF, required to be spent or encumbered by **December 31, 2024**, with final spending completion by **December 31, 2026**; the report summarizes Council-approved project allocations and **encumbered/expended** amounts as of **March 21, 2025**.
- **Council items:** Later sections list **unfunded Council resolutions/amendments** and distinguish FY 2025 amendments that **require further funding** versus those that do not.
