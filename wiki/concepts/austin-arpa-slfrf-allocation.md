Through the federal **American Rescue Plan Act of 2021 (ARPA)**, the U.S. Treasury allocated **Coronavirus State and Local Fiscal Recovery Funds (SLFRF)**. The City of Austin’s FY 2026–2030 financial forecast **ARPA update** states that Austin received **$188.5 million** in one-time SLFRF, that the full amount had to be **spent or encumbered by December 31, 2024**, and that **all ARPA spending must be completed by December 31, 2026**.

## Uses

- The City applied SLFRF across **30** distinct projects supporting residents, creatives, nonprofits, and businesses in response to **COVID-19**-related needs, per the report’s framing.

## Transparency table

- The report provides a **department / project** table with **allocated budget**, **encumbered**, and **expended** amounts as of **March 21, 2025**, spanning public health, economic development, parks, workforce, housing-related items, and other Council-approved project lines.

## Interpretation note

- Because SLFRF is **one-time**, it **does not** offset recurring General Fund or enterprise operating gaps in later years unless paired with ongoing revenue or service changes not shown in the ARPA section.
