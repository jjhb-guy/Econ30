**Austin Resource Recovery (ARR)** provides curbside collection, citywide litter abatement, diversion programs, and related services, aligned with a **Zero Waste by 2040** goal in the source document’s framing.

## Revenue and fees

- Most operating revenue comes from **utility bill charges**: **Trash Cart Fee**, **Base Customer Fee**, and **Clean Community Fee**.
- **FY 2026** operating revenue is projected up about **7.3%** versus FY 2025 budget, driven mainly by **account growth** and **planned fee increases** across the three major fees, with smaller **other revenue** growth.
- Through **FY 2030**, operating revenue is projected to rise materially versus FY 2026, again primarily through **fee and account growth** aligned with service cost recovery.

## Typical residential bill

- The “typical” residential curbside customer (64-gallon trash cart, paying all three major fees) is shown at **$40.40/month in FY 2026**, up about **4.0%** from FY 2025, with further stepped increases through FY 2030 totaling about **17.1%** cumulative from the FY 2026 level in the narrative’s cumulative figure.

## Expenditures and capital

- **FY 2026** expenditure growth (about **7.2%** vs FY 2025 budget) is attributed to **Northeast Service Center**-related **vehicle/debt transfers**, **fleet fuel and maintenance**, facility lease needs, **contracts**, and **seven** new positions (furniture reuse warehouse, expanded litter abatement, driver training).
- Out-years add **21** positions for service-area growth, more **fleet/contract** costs, and **Northeast Service Center** operating and debt costs as the facility comes online (**FY 2028** in the narrative).

## Plan vs forecast note

- The report states **minimal** overall variance between **FY 2026 Plan** and **FY 2026 Forecast** totals, while noting a small bill-level reconciliation story for the “typical” customer between plan and forecast presentations.
