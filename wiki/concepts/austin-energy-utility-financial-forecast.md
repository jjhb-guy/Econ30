**Austin Energy** is the City of Austin’s municipally owned electric utility. The FY 2026–2030 citywide financial forecast section explains how **base rates**, **pass-through rates** (including power supply and regulatory/community benefit components), **transmission revenue**, and **interest income** drive operating revenue, and how **power supply**, **wholesale transmission and ERCOT charges**, **O&M**, **debt service**, **capital investment**, and **transfers to the General Fund and citywide services** drive expenditures.

## Revenue assumptions

- **FY 2026** operating revenue is projected up about **4.5%** versus FY 2025 budget, including a **5.0% base rate increase**, higher **pass-through** revenue, partially offset by lower **other revenue** (for example a one-time **FEMA** reimbursement in FY 2025 not repeating), and higher **interest income**.
- The forecast assumes **5.0% annual base rate increases** in each out-year through FY 2030, producing large cumulative **base rate revenue** growth over the period, alongside rising **pass-through** and **transmission** revenue.

## Typical residential bill

- For a **typical residential** customer using **860 kWh/month**, the forecast shows a **FY 2026** bill of **$116.27/month**, **down** about **4.7%** from the FY 2025 budgeted bill, explained as the **net** of the proposed **5.0% base rate increase** and **cumulative reductions in the Power Supply Adjustment** since the FY 2025 budget approval. The text cautions that **future PSA / regulatory / community benefit charge** changes are not fully reflected and **additional PSA reductions** may be warranted depending on **ERCOT** market conditions.
- The illustrative path then rises to **$128.96** by FY 2030 in the published typical-bill table.

## Expenditures, transfers, and staffing

- **FY 2026** expenditures are projected up about **3.9%** versus FY 2025 budget, including higher **General Fund transfer** (from **$125 million** toward **$139 million** in the narrative), higher **city support/internal service transfers**, higher **power supply**, **O&M**, **debt service**, and **recoverable wholesale transmission/ERCOT-related** expenses.
- **Staffing:** **20** new positions in FY 2026 (focused on delivery, support, and customer energy solutions), then about **20 per year** FY 2027–FY 2030 for reliability and resiliency.

## Fund balance presentation

- The Austin Energy fund summary shows **beginning and ending balances** and small **year-to-year changes in fund balance** under the forecasted revenue and requirement paths in the report’s tables.
