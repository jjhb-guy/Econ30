The City of Austin’s **Capital Improvement Program (CIP)** is the portfolio of facility and infrastructure projects advanced across departments. The FY 2026–2030 financial forecast’s CIP section provides **historical spending context**, a **forward five-year scale** of planned spending, and an inventory of **voter-approved General Obligation bond programs** commonly referred to as **Public Improvement Bonds (PIBs)**.

## Scale and composition

- The narrative states recent citywide CIP spending has averaged about **$1.2 billion per year** over the prior five years and points to a **FY 2025–FY 2029** planning window total on the order of **$10.5 billion** in the document’s summary, with **Austin Energy**, **Austin Water**, and **Aviation** together representing about **61%** of that five-year planned spending, **PIB-funded** projects about **11%**, and other sources (including **Convention Center** and additional debt and fee-supported categories) making up the remainder.

## Funding sources (high level)

- CIP is financed from **debt** (including voter-approved GO bonds, certificates of obligation, contractual obligations, and commercial paper), **current revenues** (including fee-supported operating transfers), and **grants**, as described in the report.

## Active voter-approved GO (PIB) programs

- The report lists **four active** voter GO programs (**2016**, **2018**, **2020**, **2022**) using thresholds tied to **remaining authorization and spend** (more than **5%** authorized but unobligated and more than **10%** remaining to spend).
- A table summarizes **voter-approved amounts**, **expended-to-date** totals through **February 28, 2025**, and **percent expended** for major propositions (transportation, housing, parks, flood/open space, public safety, health and human services, etc.).
- Narrative timing expectations (based on the **FY 2025–29 CIP Plan**) indicate projected years when large bond programs approach **90% spent** of authorization for **2016**, **2018**, and **2020/2022** groupings, with a note that updated **FY 2026–30 CIP Plan** details may revise completion timing.
