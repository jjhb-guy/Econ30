The Transportation and Public Works (TPW) forecast shows growing dependence on Transportation User Fee (TUF) revenue as development-sensitive revenue streams soften.

## Revenue structure and assumptions

- FY 2026 operating revenue is projected up **$11.4 million (+5.1%)** versus FY 2025 budget, led by TUF growth and rate action.
- Forecast assumptions include a **9.0%** TUF rate increase in FY 2026 with customer growth, while Temporary Use of Right-of-Way and Traffic Impact Analysis revenues are modeled lower due to development slowdown.
- By FY 2030, revenue is projected up **$49.7 million (+21.4%)** versus FY 2026.

## Typical residential TUF bill

- Typical single-family residential TUF bill is shown at **$21.52/month in FY 2026**, up **$1.78 (+9.0%)** from FY 2025.
- Table path: **$19.74 (FY25), $21.52, $23.08, $24.29, $25.50, $26.71 (FY30)**.

## Expenditure drivers

- FY 2026 expenditures are projected up **$13.3 million (+5.9%)** versus FY 2025 budget, with citywide cost drivers, higher CIP transfers, and asset-maintenance contracts.
- By FY 2030, expenditures are projected up an additional **$39.2 million (+16.4%)** versus FY 2026, including continued citywide cost growth and increased transfers for vehicle/equipment replacement and project design.

## Combined fund trajectory

- Combined fund summary (in millions): revenue/transfers in **$232.5 (FY26)** to **$282.2 (FY30)**; expenditures/transfers out **$239.0 (FY26)** to **$278.2 (FY30)**.
- Ending balance dips in early years and then recovers to **$12.4 million** by FY 2030.
