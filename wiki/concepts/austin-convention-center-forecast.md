The Austin Convention Center Department (ACCD) forecast is shaped by the planned Convention Center closure during expansion years and the return to full operations in FY 2029.

## Core forecast dynamics

- The report describes lower revenue and expenditures during the closure window (FY 2026-FY 2028), followed by a step-up when the expanded facility reopens.
- Hotel Occupancy Tax (HOT) remains the dominant revenue source in FY 2026 (about 79% of departmental revenue in the narrative), with near-term growth assumptions moderated by a travel slowdown.

## Revenue path

- FY 2026 operating revenue is projected at **$125.4 million**, down **$9.2 million (-6.9%)** from FY 2025 budget, including lower Convention Center facility/contractor/parking revenue offset partly by Vehicle Rental Tax and Palmer events activity.
- By FY 2030, revenue is projected at **$157.4 million**, up **$32.0 million (+25.5%)** versus FY 2026, led by resumed Convention Center operations and incremental HOT and parking growth.

## Expenditures and staffing

- FY 2026 expenditures are projected at **$122.0 million**, down **$8.2 million (-6.3%)** from FY 2025 budget, including elimination of **27 vacant positions** during the closure period and higher expansion-related debt/capital transfers.
- By FY 2030, expenditures are projected at **$149.9 million**, up **$27.9 million (+22.9%)** from FY 2026, including restoration of personnel for full operations.
- FTEs in the table move from **296 (FY 2025 estimate)** to **269 (FY 2026-2027)** and back to **296** in out-years.

## Fund summary highlights

- Forecast table (in millions): revenue/transfers in **$125.4 (FY26)** to **$157.4 (FY30)**; expenditures/transfers out **$122.0 (FY26)** to **$149.9 (FY30)**.
- Ending fund balance is shown rising from **$41.0 (FY26)** to **$51.3 (FY30)**.
