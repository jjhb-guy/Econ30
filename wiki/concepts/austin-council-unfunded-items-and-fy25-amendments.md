The report includes two governance-focused follow-up sections: unresolved Council direction items and FY 2025 budget adoption amendments.

## Unfunded Council items

- The report lists approved Council resolutions for which funding was not fully identified, or where planning/research remained in progress as of **March 26, 2025**.
- Topics span climate and sustainability initiatives, cultural events and heritage support, emergency shelter continuity, public safety staffing and overtime, PFAS response, social services wage policy, parks funding strategies, downtown strategy coordination, and other implementation studies.
- The section is a tracking inventory rather than a costed five-year forecast table.

## FY25 budget adoption amendments

- The report states **15 amendments** require further funding consideration to maintain FY 2025 service levels.
- It also states **21 amendments** do **not** require further funding consideration under current assumptions (including ongoing items continuing at FY 2025 budgeted level and debt-funded items already appropriated in capital budgets).
- The table includes line-item examples with General Fund impacts (for the 15 requiring follow-up), such as homelessness-related services, EMS/public health programs, and public safety staffing-related items.

## Why this matters in the forecast

- These sections identify policy commitments and directives that may add pressure to future budgets if recurring funding sources are not adopted.
- They function as a decision backlog for future budget cycles rather than finalized appropriations for FY 2026-FY 2030.
