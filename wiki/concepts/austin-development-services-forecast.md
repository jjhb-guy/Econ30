The Development Services Department (DSD) forecast emphasizes recovery from post-peak permitting revenue declines, cost-of-service fee recalibration, and staffing right-sizing.

## Revenue context

- FY 2026 operating revenue is projected down **$2.2 million (-2.2%)** versus FY 2025 budget, reflecting lower expected development-related activity despite some fee and licensing gains.
- The narrative highlights the permitting/inspection revenue decline from **$76.5 million (FY22 peak)** to **$56.5 million (FY24 actual)**.
- By FY 2030, revenue is projected up **$38.4 million (+38.9%)** versus FY 2026, largely from fee model adjustments and moderate development recovery in later years.

## Typical residential charge

- Typical residential Clean Community Fee (Code) charge is **$4.80/month in FY 2026**, up **$0.10 (+2.1%)** from FY 2025.
- The rate path shown is **$4.80, $4.90, $5.00, $5.00, $5.00** (FY26-FY30).

## Expenditure and staffing adjustments

- FY 2026 expenditures are projected down **$2.6 million (-2.3%)** versus FY 2025 budget, including elimination of **39.85 vacant positions**, offset by base-cost pressures and targeted program additions.
- The forecast includes a graffiti abatement pilot in FY 2026 and expansion thereafter.
- By FY 2030, expenditures are projected up **$15.8 million (+14.7%)** versus FY 2026.

## Fund position signals

- Development Services Fund ending balance is shown negative through most of the horizon before returning slightly positive by FY 2030 (**$1.1 million**).
- Austin Code Fund ending balance remains positive but trends lower (**$8.1 million FY25 estimate** to **$3.1 million FY30**).
